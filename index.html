About Aisha

Aisha De Elmir is the first princess of the Elmir Empire — the oldest daughter of the imperial family, and the one who has always carried the quiet weight of a name people stopped expecting anything from.

She isn't loud, and she never has been. Where others reach for the spotlight, she stands a little to the side and watches — paying attention to the things everyone else hurries past. Her kindness comes softly, without performance. Her strength the same way: you notice it after it's already done.

The trouble is that being first wasn't ever a kindness. She grew up far from her father's attention, and far from the love that was supposed to come with the title. She learned early that a crown is just another kind of distance.

And still — the people she knows keep telling her the same thing, almost like a secret. You have a second chance now. The light is waiting.

a quiet place, kept for the brightest thing in the room

Her Story

Everyone in Elmir knows her as the first princess. But the version of her that people actually meet is quieter: the girl who was raised in the largest house in the empire, and found it almost impossible to hear her own voice in it.

There was a year — or more — that people talk about the way they talk about weather. Something closed. Something broke. No one in the palace says it plainly, but everyone knows it happened to her, and most of them are a little glad she lived through it.

And then, the night the world turned over, Aisha found herself in someone else's body. She woke up on the wrong side of her own story, and for the first time in years it looked like things could go differently.

She didn't ask for it. She just accepted it the way she accepts everything — gently, and with her eyes open. So now she walks through the light again, carrying all of it: what she lost, what she was owed, and the quiet decision to stay soft about it.

It's not a happy ending yet. But it is a beginning. And for Aisha, that's enough to keep moving.
